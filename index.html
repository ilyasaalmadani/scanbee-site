Scanner & PDF. Pay once, keep it forever.

ScanBee turns paper into clean, searchable PDFs on your iPhone. Scanning, text search, filters, folders and sharing are free with no watermark and no account. ScanBee Pro adds smart tools with a single one-time purchase: Smart Name & Folder, Due-Date Reminders, Fit-to-Size, Safe Copy, Translate, Sign and Password Lock.

Your documents stay on your iPhone. ScanBee has no account, no ads and no tracking.

Support

Restore a purchase: open ScanBee → Settings → Restore Purchase, signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy Pro.

Camera does not open: allow camera access in Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera → ScanBee.

Reminders do not appear: allow notifications in Settings → Notifications → ScanBee.

Translate asks for a download: iOS downloads each language once and then translates on the device.
