Privacy Policy

ScanBee · Effective 2 October 2026

Summary

ScanBee does not collect, store or share any personal data. Everything you scan stays on your iPhone.

What ScanBee processes, and where

  • Scans, photos and PDFs you add are stored only in ScanBee's storage on your device. They are included in your own iPhone or iCloud backup if you use one. ScanBee never uploads them to us or to anyone else.
  • Text recognition, edge detection, Smart Name, date detection, Safe Copy and signatures run entirely on your device using Apple frameworks. When your iPhone supports Apple Intelligence, Smart Name uses Apple's on-device model.
  • Translate uses Apple's Translation framework on your device. The first time you use a language, iOS may download that language from Apple.
  • Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device.
  • Camera and Photos are used only when you choose to scan or import.

Purchases

ScanBee Pro is a one-time In-App Purchase processed by Apple. We receive no payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to the transaction.

No tracking, no analytics, no ads

ScanBee contains no third-party SDKs, advertising, analytics or tracking, and requires no account.

Deleting your data

Delete documents inside ScanBee, or delete the app to remove all of its data from your iPhone.
